## Satish Subrahmanya Gonella — Senior AI Architect

I build AI systems that survive contact with production. That means agentic workflows, retrieval and evaluation that hold up under real users, real data and real audits.

### What I work on

- **Multi-agent orchestration** on durable workflows (Temporal), with human approval on anything irreversible
- **Structured outputs** validated before anything executes, and multi-model routing across Anthropic, Azure OpenAI and open-source models
- **RAG** that filters on permissions before it ranks, and cites its sources
- **Evaluation gates** that block a bad release: golden sets, regression suites, mutation testing, prompt-injection testing
- **The platform underneath**: Kubernetes, Terraform, Azure and AWS, CI/CD, observability

### Background

Twenty years in engineering, more than a decade of it inside tier-one UK banks. Azure Solutions Architect Expert, AWS Solutions Architect Professional, HashiCorp Terraform and Vault.
